Questions tradies usually ask

Quick answers before you test the demo.

Do I need to download an app?

No. TradieCatch can run in a modern web browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Can customers leave a voicemail?

Yes. Callers can press 1 to leave a voicemail, or hang up and receive the SMS follow-up.

Can I edit my services?

Yes. You can edit your service list from Settings so the SMS bot matches your business.

What happens if the customer is too far away?

If service-area checking is enabled, the bot can flag out-of-area enquiries for manual review instead of auto-booking them.
